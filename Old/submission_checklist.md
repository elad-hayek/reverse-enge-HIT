# Submission Checklist for a Two-Person Team

This checklist is derived from the two supplied assignment Markdown files. The
syllabus specifies the deliverables and email rules, but it does not prescribe
one mandatory internal ZIP folder tree. The structure below is a clear,
conservative packaging recommendation.

## Required Delivery Method

- Submit by email only to the address stated in the syllabus.
- Do not use Google Drive, Dropbox, or the course drive.
- The main report must be DOCX or ODT, not PDF.
- The source collection may be sent as ZIP/RAR and may be split across several
  emails if attachment limits require it.
- Every email subject should begin with `RCE2026B` and include both students'
  names and ID numbers.
- The email body should include, for both students: Hebrew name, English name,
  ID number, phone, email, and lecture group/day/hour.

The source PDFs should remain separate files. Do not combine all articles into
one PDF because the syllabus asks for each article separately.

## Before Creating the Archive

- Fill both student rows in the report.
- Replace all five placeholder PNG files with genuine diagrams or screenshots.
- Preserve every cited page S1-S45 as a PDF print or complete scrolling
  screenshot.
- Include the significant AI prompt sequence, raw responses, text copies, tool
  name/version, and screenshots.
- Confirm every URL and citation.
- Convert the finished Markdown files to DOCX where appropriate.
- Open the generated DOCX files and check tables, images, headings, and links.

## Suggested Email Split

If one message is too large:

1. Email 1: main DOCX, source list, and AI log.
2. Email 2: `Articles_S01-S22.zip`.
3. Email 3: `Articles_S23-S45_and_Task2.zip`.

Use the same subject identification in every message and label them clearly as
`Part 1 of 3`, `Part 2 of 3`, and `Part 3 of 3`.
